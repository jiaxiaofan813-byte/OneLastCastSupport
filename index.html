Find your rhythm

A little patience.
A very good second tap.

The hook casts automatically. Watch the fish beneath your boat, choose your moment, and bring the catch home.

01

Tap to dive

Send the hook straight down. The first fish it actually touches is the one you catch.

02

Meet the ring

Tap again when the shrinking ring meets the target. Small fish are more forgiving; bigger fish test your timing.

03

Keep casting

Land the fish in your basket and the next cast begins. A missed catch ends the day, but a new day is one tap away.

Your own fish log

Small catches. Lasting collection.

Discover 20 illustrated freshwater species. Every fish that reaches the basket adds to your on-device Fish Log, across all your fishing days.

A lake in your pocket

Settle in. Cast again.

Play without an account or an internet connection. Adjust music, sound effects, haptics and motion in Settings. Switch between English and Chinese without resetting your collection.
