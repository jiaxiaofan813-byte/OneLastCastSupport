One Last Cast

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Developer: Xiaofan Jia

The game works offline. No account is required, and the app does not send your gameplay or personal data to the developer.

What the app stores

One Last Cast stores game settings, language preference, fish collection counts, best score and combo, and a checkpoint for your current fishing day in the app’s local storage on your device. Local identifiers used to prevent duplicate catch records stay in that storage. The app does not upload these records.

What the app does not collect

The app does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It includes no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, account sign-in, Game Center integration, or developer-operated cloud sync. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library. Music and artwork are bundled with the app.

Your device and backups

Your records remain in this installation’s app data until that data is removed. iOS or iPadOS may include app data in backups or device transfers according to your Apple settings. Those system services are provided by Apple, not a cloud-save service operated by One Last Cast.

Deleting the app through the system’s Delete App option removes its local app data. Offloading the app can retain its documents and data. Existing backups are managed separately in your device or Apple account settings and may restore previously saved data. There is no account to delete and no in-app clear-all-data control in this version. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your Fish Log.

If you contact support

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and the information or attachments you send, for handling your request. That information is separate from gameplay data and is processed through the email service. Share only what is needed, such as the app version, device model, operating-system version and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information. You can contact the same address to request deletion of your support correspondence.

This website and external services

This policy distinguishes the offline game from its website. The website does not include developer-added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links and email services follow their own policies. Apple may also handle App Store purchases, downloads and optional diagnostics under its policies and your settings.

Children

The app does not ask children to provide personal information. A parent or guardian should help a child contact support and avoid sending the child’s personal details.

Changes and contact

This page will identify the effective date of any updated policy. Questions about privacy or support can be sent to Xiaofan Jia at jiaxiaofan813@gmail.com.
