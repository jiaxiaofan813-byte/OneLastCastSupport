Playing & progress

How do the two taps work?

The hook casts automatically. Tap once to make it dive toward a visible fish, then tap again when the shrinking ring meets the target ring. Holding the screen or releasing a touch is not an additional action.

Do I really have to wait until tomorrow?

No. “Come Back Tomorrow” starts a new fishing day immediately. Your lifetime Fish Log and best records remain; a new day starts a fresh run.

Why did a catch not count?

Only a real fish that lands in the basket adds to the Fish Log. A fish merely touched by the hook does not count yet. Snails and weeds are rescue objects, not fish, and do not add fish counts or points.

Can I move my Fish Log between devices?

This version has no account or in-app cloud sync. Records are stored in this installation’s app data. Apple system backups or device transfers may include them, depending on your settings; the developer cannot retrieve a copy from a game server.

Settings & troubleshooting

Why is there no sound?

Check Background Music and the music/sound-effect volume in Settings, then check the device volume, silent mode and selected audio output. The game respects silent mode. After removing headphones or an audio interruption, use Start or Resume to continue audio.

How do I change language or motion?

Open Settings and choose English or 中文. You can also adjust music, sound effects, Haptic Feedback and Reduce Motion. Haptics require supported hardware.

What if a save warning appears?

Keep the app open so pending fish records can retry, and check that your device has free storage. If the warning remains, contact support with its exact text. Do not delete or reinstall the app if you want to preserve local progress.

How do I remove my records?

There is no in-app clear-all-data control. Deleting the app removes its local data, while offloading can retain it. Backups are separate. Read the privacy policy before deleting anything.

Privacy & feedback

The game runs offline and does not send gameplay data to the developer. If you email support, only include details needed for the issue. A parent or guardian should help children send feedback. See the privacy policy.
